### 1. Professional Background and Career Path  
Salimage, aged 32, is a customer advisor at La Banque Postale. After obtaining a two-year vocational diploma in sales and spending one year at business school, he completed a nine-month work-study training program, which enabled him to obtain a professional skills certificate as a financial advisor.

### 2. Career Development  
After completing his training, he worked for three years as a financial advisor at a post office. He then became a customer advisor, a position he has held for the past five years.

### 3. Main Responsibilities  
He manages a portfolio of 1,600 customers, including 500 key customers who represent the core of his business. His responsibilities include welcoming, advising and retaining customers, while offering them savings, financing, insurance and wealth management solutions tailored to their needs.

### 4. Work Organization  
Each day begins with approximately 45 minutes spent preparing for appointments. He meets an average of six to seven customers per day, with interviews lasting between 45 minutes and one hour. He must also answer calls, follow up on applications, develop his customer portfolio and keep up to date with economic and financial news.

### 5. Qualities and Advice for Succeeding  
He particularly enjoys this profession because of the human contact and the variety of situations he encounters. To work in this field, he recommends being persistent and enjoying challenges, but above all, being able to listen to and understand customers’ needs. In his view, the quality of the advice provided is essential for standing out.
